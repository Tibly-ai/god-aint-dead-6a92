The clothes are
the flyer

01

Scripture on every piece

No vague slogans. Chapter and verse sits on the sleeve, the hem or across the back, printed big enough to read from across the room.

02

Built heavy on purpose

Tees are 240 gsm ringspun cotton. Hoodies are brushed fleece with double-stitched cuffs. These are meant to be worn hard for years, not folded away.

03

Small runs, printed by us

Fifty pieces or fewer per design, pulled by hand in Nashville. Slight ink variation is part of it. Nothing is mass produced overseas.
