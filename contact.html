Pick the lane so it hits the right desk

  1. 01

    Wholesale

    Boutiques, record stores, skate shops and Christian bookstores. Minimum is 24 pieces per style, mixed sizes welcome. Ask for the line sheet and current wholesale pricing.

  2. 02

    Church & ministry

    Youth groups, camps, worship nights, street teams. We print your verse and your name on the same heavyweight blanks we use for our own drops. Send the date you need them in hand and we work backward.

  3. 03

    Collabs

    Bands, tattoo artists, illustrators, photographers. Show us the work first. If the art carries the Gospel and looks like it was nailed to a door, we want to talk about a limited run.

  4. 04

    Order questions

    Tracking, sizing, exchanges within 30 days on unworn pieces. Include your order number and the email you checked out with so we can pull it fast.
