The Mission Psalm 18:2 / The Lord is my rock

The name is
the whole
sermon.

We started God Ain't Dead in a Nashville garage with one press, a stack of blank heavyweight tees and a stubborn conviction: people read shirts. They read them in line at the gas station, in the pit at a show, across a bus aisle at 6am. A shirt says something before you open your mouth.

So we print the loudest thing we know. He is alive. The tomb is still empty. That claim is two thousand years old and it has outlasted every empire that tried to bury it, which is exactly why the artwork looks like a flyer stapled to a telephone pole. Gothic type, hard rules, blood-red ink. Nothing soft about it.

Every graphic starts with an open Bible, not a mood board. We pull the verse first, sit with it for a week, then draw. If the design cannot carry the verse, the design gets scrapped. We have thrown out more shirts than we have sold.

The source material

Six verses
we keep
printing.

These are the passages the whole catalog is built on. Each one has become a graphic, and a few have been redrawn three or four times because we got the drawing wrong before we got it right. Read them before you buy anything. The shirt is just a delivery method.

  1. 01

    Romans 1:16

    Not Ashamed hoodie

    The founding verse. Shame is the quietest way faith dies, so we put the opposite across the chest in blackletter big enough to read from across a parking lot.

  2. 02

    John 11:25

    Resurrection tee

    "I am the resurrection and the life." Printed over an empty tomb cut from a woodcut engraving. This is the one people stop us about in the street.

  3. 03

    Psalm 23:4

    Valley long sleeve

    Written for the people wearing it through a hard year. Sleeve print runs wrist to elbow so you read it every time you look at your hands.

  4. 04

    Ephesians 6:11

    Armor work jacket

    Heavy canvas, chain-stitched back panel. The armor language is not decoration. It is the reason we build the outerwear to survive a decade of wear.

  5. 05

    Isaiah 53:5

    Pierced crewneck

    The only design we print with a single spot of red ink and nothing else. It took eleven proofs to stop it from feeling cheap. Restraint was the whole job.

  6. 06

    Matthew 28:6

    He Is Risen cap

    Four words on the front panel. It is the plainest thing we make and it outsells everything else we have ever pressed.

If the verse is not on the tag, the shirt does not leave the studio.

Every garment ships with the full passage printed on the inside neck label and a folded card with the chapter around it. Someone is going to ask you what your shirt means. We want you to have the words ready.

Where the money goes

A tenth off the top, before anything else.

Ten percent of every order leaves the account the week it lands, before ink, blanks or rent get paid. Nobody here takes a salary out of that tenth. We post the totals on Instagram at the end of each drop so you can hold us to it.

Street outreach

Winter coats, socks and hot meals handed out downtown with a Nashville church team every other Friday night.

Prison Bibles

Study Bibles and letter postage for a chaplaincy program working across three Tennessee facilities.

Youth rooms

Sound gear and van fuel for small youth ministries that run on volunteers and secondhand everything.
