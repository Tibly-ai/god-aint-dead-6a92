Lookbook Worn Loud

Six looks, photographed in one afternoon between the church steps on 14th and the loading docks behind the studio. No studio lighting, no retouching. Every graphic on this page comes from a verse, and we print the reference on the inside label so you can find it later.

The Collection
On The Street

Heavyweight cotton, boxy cuts, screen printed in runs of fifty. Tap any photo to see it full screen. Sizes run S through 3XL and every piece here is either in stock or back within two weeks.

Look 01

Not Ashamed Tee

Romans 1:16

"I am not ashamed of the gospel." Cracked blackletter across the chest, 8oz cotton, printed twice so the ink sits heavy.

Look 02

Valley Hood

Psalm 23:4

"Though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil." Sleeve print runs wrist to elbow.

Look 03

Living Water Longsleeve

John 4:14

"A spring of water welling up to eternal life." Bone print on ink, washed once before it ships so it stops shrinking.

Look 04

Armor Coach Jacket

Ephesians 6:11

"Put on the full armor of God." Snap front, lined body, full back print of a shield rendered in the same cracked type as the tees. This one took three passes on the press to get the black deep enough.

Look 05

Stone Rolled Crew

Matthew 28:6

"He is not here; he has risen." Small blood-red chest hit, big statement across the back.

Look 06

Light In The Dark Cap

John 1:5

"The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it." Unstructured six-panel, bone stitch.
